# E-Commerce Sales & Delivery Dashboard
### An Excel-Based Analysis of Sales Performance, Customer Behaviour, and Delivery Operations

**Project Type:** Data Analytics – Excel Mini Project  
**Program:** Imarticus Postgraduate Program in Data Science and Analytics with GenAI  
**Tools:** Microsoft Excel  
**Dataset:** 500 Orders · 100 Customers · 50 Products  
**Development Approach:** Guided Academic Project with AI Assistance

---

## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing an e-commerce store's sales and delivery data using Microsoft Excel.

The objective was to transform raw order-level data into meaningful business information through data cleaning, calculated columns, PivotTables, KPI calculations, and an interactive dashboard.

The analysis explores different aspects of the store's operations, including sales performance, customer demographics, product performance, coupon usage, geographical sales distribution, and delivery efficiency.

The final output is an interactive Excel dashboard that allows users to explore different business metrics and filter the results using slicers.

## 2. Business Problem

An e-commerce business generates large amounts of transactional data through customer orders.

However, raw order records alone do not provide an immediate understanding of business performance.

A business needs to understand questions such as:

- What is the total sales revenue generated?
- How much revenue is reduced through discounts?
- Which cities contribute the most to sales?
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- How frequently are discount coupons used?
- How efficiently are orders delivered?
- Which customer groups contribute to sales?

The objective of this project was to organize the available data and develop an Excel dashboard that helps answer these questions.

## 3. Dataset Overview

The project uses a synthetic e-commerce dataset provided as part of the Imarticus training module.

The dataset contains:

- 500 customer orders.
- 100 unique customers.
- 50 unique products.
- Customer demographic information.
- Product and order details.
- Product prices and quantities.
- Coupon codes and delivery charges.
- Order and delivery dates.

The dataset was organized into an Excel workbook containing separate sheets for raw data, cleaned working data, PivotTables, KPIs, and the final dashboard.

## 4. Data Cleaning and Preparation

The first stage involved preparing the dataset for analysis.

To maintain the original records, I kept the raw data sheet unchanged and performed the cleaning and preparation work in a separate working data sheet.

I also created calculated columns to support additional analysis.

One example was calculating the order amount after accounting for delivery charges.

This prepared dataset became the foundation for the subsequent PivotTables, KPI calculations, and visualizations.

## 5. Data Analysis and Business Metrics

After preparing the data, I created seven PivotTables to analyze different dimensions of the business.

### Sales Performance

Analyzed overall sales figures, discounts, and delivery charges to understand the store's revenue performance.

### Geographical Analysis

Compared sales and order volumes across different cities to identify geographical differences in business performance.

### Monthly Sales Trends

Analyzed sales across different months to identify periods with higher and lower sales performance.

### Product Performance

Compared products based on order frequency and revenue generation to identify popular products and high-revenue products.

### Customer Analysis

Examined customer-related information, including gender and age, to understand the distribution of customers.

### Coupon Analysis

Analyzed coupon usage to understand the frequency of different promotional codes.

### Delivery Performance

Examined delivery time and delivery charges to understand the operational side of order fulfilment.

## 6. KPI Development

A separate KPI sheet was created to consolidate the main business metrics.

The key metrics included:

- Total orders placed.
- Unique customers and products.
- Total sales before discounts.
- Total discounts provided.
- Sales after discounts.
- Revenue after discounts and delivery charges.
- Average delivery time.
- Orders with zero delivery charges.
- Average customer age.

These KPIs provided a summarized view of the store's overall performance.

## 7. Interactive Dashboard

The final stage involved building an interactive dashboard using Excel charts and slicers.

The dashboard contains 12 charts and 2 slicers, allowing users to explore the data through different visual perspectives.

The dashboard brings together the results of the PivotTables and KPI calculations into a single interface.

This makes it easier to understand sales performance, compare business categories, and explore the underlying data without manually navigating through multiple sheets.

## 8. Key Findings and Business Observations

The analysis produced several notable findings.

**1. Geographical Sales Performance**

Houston recorded the highest order volume with 80 orders and generated approximately 60,100 in sales. Miami recorded the lowest order volume with 44 orders and approximately 24,800 in sales.

**2. Monthly Sales Variation**

April recorded the highest monthly sales at approximately 38,600, while May recorded the lowest at approximately 22,800.

**3. Product Performance**

PID036 was the most frequently ordered product, with 17 orders. However, PID027 generated the highest revenue at approximately 18,900.

This highlights the difference between product popularity and revenue contribution.

**4. Coupon Usage**

Coupon usage was relatively distributed across the five coupon codes.

FLAT8 was used in 111 orders, followed by FLAT20 in 104 orders, FLAT10 in 100 orders, DEL0 in 102 orders, and FLAT15 in 83 orders.

**5. Delivery Performance**

The average delivery time was approximately four days across the 500 orders.

Additionally, 102 orders had zero delivery charges.
